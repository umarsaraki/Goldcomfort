Last updated August 2026

🔒 Registered & accountable

GoldComfort is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and operates under Nigeria's NDPR data protection law. Confirmed fraud is reported directly to the EFCC and the Nigeria Police.

1. Identity verification

Every reseller must provide a valid NIN before listing any product. This is checked against NIMC records, together with a real photo, to confirm you are who you say you are. We do not sell or share this data — it exists only to verify identity and to trace accountability if something goes wrong.

2. How payments work

When a customer pays for an order, GoldComfort holds the funds. They are only released to the reseller after the customer's delivery is confirmed by the reseller in their dashboard. This protects customers from paying for goods that never arrive.

3. What counts as fraud

  • Taking payment for a product you don't have or won't deliver
  • Using another person's NIN or identity documents to register
  • Deliberately misrepresenting a product's condition, quantity, or price
  • Creating multiple accounts to evade a ban

Any of the above results in an immediate, permanent ban. Because your verified identity is on file, confirmed cases are reported to the EFCC and Nigeria Police with the evidence we hold.

4. Delivery area & order limits

Resellers declare which states they deliver to. Orders from outside that coverage are declined automatically at checkout — this is disclosed to customers before they pay. New resellers are limited to ₦20,000 per order until 5 orders are completed cleanly.

5. Data protection (NDPR)

Your personal data is processed only for identity verification, fraud prevention, and platform operation, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation. You may request a copy of your data or its deletion, subject to our legal obligation to retain fraud-related records.

6. Fees

Reseller plan fees are billed monthly in advance and are non-refundable once identity verification has started, since that check has a real cost to GoldComfort regardless of outcome.
